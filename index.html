Everything You Need to Know

Clear, honest answers about privacy, encryption, and the architecture of Orelio.

Yes, entirely. Every line of TypeScript, HTML, and CSS is published on GitHub. There are no premium tiers, no hidden subscription fees, and no in-app purchases. You can clone the repository, run it offline on your own machine, or contribute improvements.
Your financial portfolio data is stored locally on your machine in your browser's persistent storage and your local JSON database. Orelio does not have cloud databases, backend storage APIs, or user authentication servers.
When you set or update your master password, Orelio hashes and derives cryptographic keys client-side using PBKDF2. When you log out, your ledger state is immediately cleared from active memory and sealed. Only your correct master password can unlock your dashboard.
Our upcoming cross-platform sync utilizes direct OAuth authorization to your personal Google Drive account. Your ledger file is encrypted client-side before syncing. Orelio never operates intermediary relay servers — data transfers strictly between your device and your personal Drive storage.
Yes. Orelio supports integrated CAS ingestion for both CDSL and NSDL demat statements, as well as CAMS / KFintech consolidated account statements. Parsing occurs client-side in memory on your device.
